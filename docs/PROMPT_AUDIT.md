# Prompt audit: the original Grinda brief

Scope: the owner's initial build request (one message). Method: static analysis with the `prompt-audit` skill (five dimensions). No behavioural evaluation. Assumptions: "stack based" means "stake based"; "Steppa: Schritte-Challenges" is the iOS app id6755126064.

Each finding lists how the build resolves it.

## Contradictions

- **WARNING**: "use psychology tricks ... to make them pay" vs. "build around trust ... someone enters the app, should feel like finally i got an app that cares".
  Manipulative tricks erode trust once noticed, and in a money app one noticed trick ends the relationship. Failure mode: short-term conversion, then 1-star reviews ("scam").
  **Resolution:** Use Nir Eyal's own Manipulation Matrix: only build hooks the maker would use themselves and that materially improve the user's life ("facilitator" quadrant). All hooks listed in `docs/STRATEGY.md` pass this test. No fake timers, no fake scarcity, no hidden fees.

- **WARNING**: "the thing is, people should trust" vs. "create statistics, showing we helped XYZ people lose weight".
  Before launch there are no users, so any number would be fabricated. That violates the EU UCPD, US FTC Act §5, and App Store guideline 2.3.1.
  **Resolution:** A real `public_stats` database view (steps walked, km, stakes refunded, challenges completed, self-reported weight lost with opt-in) plus a share-card generator that only reads that view.

## Ambiguity

- **ERROR** (Term): "stack based app for steps". Read as "stake based": users stake money on challenges. Every other part of the brief supports this reading.
- **ERROR** (Scope): "build me an iphone app which i can just pull it up on app store". Shipping needs an Apple Developer account, code signing, App Store Connect metadata, and App Review, and this machine runs Linux, not macOS.
  **Resolution:** A complete Xcode project generated by XcodeGen, CI on GitHub macOS runners, and a fastlane `release` lane. The owner adds App Store Connect API key secrets. See `docs/SHIPPING.md`.
- **WARNING** (Scope): "connected to trackers like health app, and others". Apple Health already aggregates Apple Watch, Garmin, Fitbit (via sync apps), Oura, Withings, and others.
  **Resolution:** HealthKit is v1, and the app tells users how to connect other trackers through Health. Direct Garmin/Fitbit APIs are on the roadmap.
- **WARNING** (Term): "make sure the name, logo, is actually like a fortune 500 logo". Resolved as design principles: see `brand/BRAND.md`.
- **INFO** (Quantifier): "make some stuff free but make sure we wont have just a BS app that can work flawlessly without payment". Resolved as: the free tier tracks and motivates; commitment (the stake) is the paid core. See `docs/STRATEGY.md`, "Free vs. paid".

## Persona issues

- **WARNING**: The brief says both "not around making you pay" and "make them pay ... thats the indirect main goal". A voice that is part coach, part casino confuses users.
  **Resolution:** One persona only, the coach. Copy talks about walking, streaks, and the body. Money talk is factual and appears only where money moves.

## Cognitive load

Overall complexity: **High**. The brief bundles product strategy, brand identity, legal-sensitive payments, backend, iOS engineering, CI/CD, marketing, and git hygiene.
- **Constraint Overload**: Resolved by splitting the work into phases with a commit per step (plan, brand, iOS shell, features, backend, CI, screenshots, shipping docs).

## Semantic coverage

Overall coverage: **Limited**. Vision is clear; operational and legal edges are missing.

### Coverage gaps (resolved in build)
- **Apple payments policy.** Real-money stakes are not a digital good (guideline 3.1.1). Commitment apps with Stripe pre-auth (e.g. Forfeit) are live on the App Store. Stakes go through Stripe; the Pro subscription goes through StoreKit.
- **Gambling law.** A prize pool funded by losers is a contest. The owner chose "we keep forfeits": a commitment contract with no chance element and no prize. Legal review per market is still required (DE: consumer-law limits on contractual penalties, BGB §309 Nr. 6 / §343).
- **Cheating.** Steppa reviewers complain about "20k steps within hours". Mitigation: user-entered samples are ignored, cadence plausibility checks, and App Attest.
- **Settlement delays.** Steppa's top complaint is 1 to 2 days to settle. Grinda settles 3 hours after the day closes and releases holds instantly.
- **Health claims.** No medical claims. Show a disclaimer, and estimate kcal with a transparent formula.
- **Minors.** Staking requires age 18+.
- **Account deletion** (App Store 5.1.1(v)): included in-app.

### Missing error handling (resolved in build)
- HealthKit permission denied: the app explains why it is needed, links to Settings, and blocks staking until access is granted.
- Health data syncs late (e.g. Garmin): a 3-hour grace window after day close before settlement.
- Payment fails or the hold expires: challenge not activated; a hold nearing expiry is converted to a capture-then-refund flow.
- Steps sync but the device is offline at day end: the device uploads on next launch; the server settles on the latest submission within grace.
