Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

What we read

With your permission, Steppie reads steps, walking distance and body weight from Apple Health. We use them only to track your races and show your progress. We keep daily and hourly step totals for your races to settle them and prevent cheating.

What we never do

Health data is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never used for advertising or tracking. Steppie contains no ad or tracking SDKs.

Account and payments

You sign in with Apple, so we may receive your name and a private relay email. Card payments and cash-outs are processed by Whop; Steppie never sees or stores your card number. We keep the Whop payment and transfer references, and the Whop account a cash-out is sent to. Subscriptions are handled by Apple.

Age and place

Before your first paid race we ask for your date of birth and the country (and in the US, Canada and Australia, the state) you live in. Paid races are only open to adults, and some places don't allow them, so we use these two answers only to decide whether paid races are open to you. They are saved once; if you move, write to support.

Screenshots and reviews

If you send a Health screenshot to show a day you walked, a person at Steppie looks at it to decide that day. It is stored privately and only used for that review.

Where data lives

Account and race data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. Step data used for the home-screen widget stays on your device.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app (You › Delete account). Unpaid checkouts are called off first. A race that is still running has to settle before the account can go, because other people's purses depend on it. Money in your Vault is paid out to you or, if you choose, given up. We keep payment and ledger records without your name or account, because bookkeeping rules require it. For access or correction requests under GDPR, email privacy@steppie.app.

Health data legal basis (GDPR Art. 9)

We process health data on the basis of your explicit consent, given in the Apple Health permission prompt, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access.
