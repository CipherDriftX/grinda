My steps didn't count

Steppie uses the total that Apple Health shows. Open Health › Steps and check the day. If your watch or tracker synced late, Steppie waits 3 hours after midnight before settling. If a verified sync problem caused a miss, email us within 48 hours and we'll fix it.

When do I get my money back?

When a solo race you finished settles (3 hours after its last day closes), the card part is refunded to your card through Whop and the Vault part goes straight back to your Vault. A card refund can take several working days to show, depending on your bank. Purse winnings land in your Vault when the purse settles, 24 hours after its last day. Cash out from 5 in the Vault.

How do I connect Garmin, Fitbit or Oura?

Let your tracker's app share steps with Apple Health. Steppie reads from Health, so they count automatically.
