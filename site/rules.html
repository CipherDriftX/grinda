Race rules

Last updated 25 October 2026

Solo races

  1. A race has a daily step goal, a number of days, and a stake you choose.
  2. A day counts when Apple Health shows at least the goal for that calendar day in the time zone you started in. Manually entered steps are excluded.
  3. Each day closes at midnight. Settlement runs 3 hours after the last day closes so delayed syncs are included.
  4. Grace days, where included, automatically cover missed days, in order. Shields you equip when entering (up to 2 per race) cover further missed days. Unused Shields return to your locker after settlement.
  5. Races of 5 days or fewer place an authorisation (hold) on your card. If you finish, the hold is released and nothing is charged. If you miss, the hold is captured.
  6. Longer races charge the stake when you enter. If you finish, the full stake is refunded to the original payment method automatically.
  7. If you miss a day beyond your grace days and Shields, the stake is kept by Steppie.
  8. Comeback: within 72 hours of a missed staked race you may enter one 5-day Comeback race at the same daily goal, with a stake at least equal to the missed one. If you finish it, your Comeback stake is returned in full and 50% of the missed stake is refunded to its original payment method. A Comeback cannot have a Comeback.
  9. Whether money comes back depends only on your steps. Grit, leagues, stake tables and Shoe Boxes are for fun, have no cash value and cannot be bought or exchanged.
  10. Champion stakes (50 and up) open after one finished staked race, Legend stakes (100 and up) after three.
  11. Step data that looks physically impossible is reviewed by a person. A day under review is never marked as missed automatically.
  12. If a verified sync problem caused a miss, contact support within 48 hours and we'll fix it.
  13. Staked races are available to adults 18 and over, where permitted by local law.

Purse races

  1. A purse has a daily step goal, a number of days, a start date and a fixed entry. Some purses only count steps inside a time window, shown on the purse.
  2. When you join, you pay the entry plus a service fee of 0.50 plus 4% of the entry. Steppie Pro members pay no service fee. Host purses add 5% of the entry for the host. Entry credit is used first, then your Vault balance, then your card.
  3. A day counts when Apple Health shows at least the goal for that calendar day in your time zone. Each day closes at midnight. A purse settles 24 hours after its last day closes, so late syncs are included.
  4. Shields you equip when joining (up to 2) cover missed days, as in solo races. Unused Shields return to your locker.
  5. Finish every day and you finish the purse. Miss a day beyond your Shields and you are out; your entry stays in the purse.
  6. 100% of the purse is split evenly between everyone who finishes. Leftover cents go one each to the finishers who joined first. Steppie keeps only the service fee.
  7. If nobody finishes, every entry goes back to its owner's Vault.
  8. If a day was marked missed but you walked it, send a Health screenshot from the race page before the purse settles. A person checks it, and the purse waits for the answer.
  9. Accounts using fake or added steps are removed and their entries stay in the purse. We never name them.
  10. Winnings go to your Vault. You can cash out from 25, up to 500 a day.
  11. Paid purses are available to adults 18 and over, where permitted by local law.
