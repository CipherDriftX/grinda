# Grinda

**Walk it off. Keep your money.**

Grinda is an iPhone app that turns a daily walking goal into a commitment backed by your own money. Stake an amount on a challenge, hit your steps, and get every cent back. Miss, and the stake is kept. Built to help people lose weight by walking, not to take their money.

- `PRODUCT.md`: product record
- `docs/`: research, strategy, prompt audit, shipping guide
- `brand/`: name, logo, brand guide
- `ios/`: SwiftUI app (XcodeGen project)
- `supabase/`: database, Edge Functions (Stripe, settlement, public stats)
- `screenshots/`: Simulator screenshots from CI
