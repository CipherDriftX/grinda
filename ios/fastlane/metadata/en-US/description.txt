Back yourself. Keep your money.

Steppie turns your daily walking goal into a commitment backed by your own money. Pick a race, like 8,000 steps a day for 5 days. Put down a stake. Walk. When you finish, every cent comes back.

It works because losing money hurts more than winning feels good. In a large study of step challenges with money on the line, people walked about 31% more, and three in four hit their goal.

HOLD, DON'T CHARGE
Races of 5 days or fewer only place a hold on your card. Finish, and the hold is released. Nothing is ever charged.

MONEY BACK IN MINUTES
Longer races are refunded in full, automatically, within minutes of settling. No withdrawal limits, no waiting days.

MEET THE CREW
Steppie is a lion in running shoes who runs your laps on the track. Dash the cheetah paces you all day. Shelly the tortoise keeps your Shields. Pip the sparrow brings the news. Bo the bear guards the Vault.

MAKE IT A GAME
Pick a stake table from Rookie to Legend: bigger stakes earn more Grit in the weekly league. Equip up to two Shields to cover a bad day; unused ones come back. Missed a race? One Comeback within 72 hours wins back half. Every finish opens a Shoe Box with new trainers for Steppie. Whether your money comes back depends only on your steps.

EVERY CENT IN VIEW
The Vault shows every hold, charge, release and refund with its Stripe reference. We publish our own books too: how much we've returned and how much we've kept.

BUILT AROUND YOUR BODY
Tell Steppie where you're starting and where you want to be. Your goal comes from your real step average, not a random 10,000. See steps turn into kilometres, calories and fat walked off.

WORKS WITH YOUR TRACKER
Steppie reads steps from Apple Health, so iPhone, Apple Watch, Garmin, Fitbit, Oura, Withings and Samsung all count. Hand-typed steps don't, which keeps races fair for everyone.

FREE, AND ACTUALLY USEFUL
Step track, streaks, weight trend, practice laps, widgets and share cards are free forever.

STEPPIE PRO
Custom races, up to three races at once, friend races, deeper insights and the Stride S app icon.

HOW WE MAKE MONEY
From stakes on missed races, Shields and Steppie Pro. We'd rather you finish, so everything in the app is built to get you over the line.

Staked races are for adults 18+. Steppie is a motivation tool, not medical advice. Calorie and fat figures are estimates.
