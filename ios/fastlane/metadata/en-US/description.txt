Walk it off. Keep your money.

Grinda turns your daily walking goal into a commitment backed by your own money. Pick a race, like 8,000 steps a day for 5 days. Put down a stake. Walk. When you finish, every cent comes back.

It works because losing money hurts more than winning feels good. In a large study of step challenges with money on the line, people walked about 31% more, and three in four hit their goal.

HOLD, DON'T CHARGE
Races of 5 days or fewer only place a hold on your card. Finish, and the hold is released. Nothing is ever charged.

MONEY BACK IN MINUTES
Longer races are refunded in full, automatically, within minutes of settling. No withdrawal limits, no waiting days.

EVERY CENT IN VIEW
Your Wallet shows every hold, charge, release and refund with its Stripe reference. We publish our own books too: how much we've returned and how much we've kept.

BUILT AROUND YOUR BODY
Tell Grinda where you're starting and where you want to be. Your goal comes from your real step average, not a random 10,000. See steps turn into kilometres, calories and fat walked off.

WORKS WITH YOUR TRACKER
Grinda reads steps from Apple Health, so iPhone, Apple Watch, Garmin, Fitbit, Oura, Withings and Samsung all count. Hand-typed steps don't, which keeps races fair for everyone.

FREE, AND ACTUALLY USEFUL
Step track, streaks, weight trend, practice laps, widgets and share cards are free forever.

GRINDA PRO
Grace tokens, custom races, up to three races at once, friend races and deeper insights.

HOW WE MAKE MONEY
From stakes on missed races, and from Grinda Pro. We'd rather you finish, so everything in the app is built to get you over the line.

Staked races are for adults 18+. Grinda is a motivation tool, not medical advice. Calorie and fat figures are estimates.
