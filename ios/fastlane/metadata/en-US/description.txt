Get paid to walk.

Join a purse, hit your daily steps, and split the pot with everyone who finishes. Or race yourself: put down a stake, walk, and get every cent back when you finish.

It works because money on the line moves people. In a study of nearly 73,000 step challenges with a deposit at stake, people walked about 31% more, and nearly three in four hit their goal.

PURSES
Pick a purse, like 10,000 steps a day for 7 days, with an entry from $5 to $250. Finish every day and you split 100% of the purse with the other finishers. Miss a day and your entry stays in for them. You see the fee and your possible split before you pin. If nobody finishes, everyone gets their entry back.

HOST YOUR OWN
Start a crew purse for friends, family or your team, free. Public purses earn the host 5% of every entry.

SOLO RACES
Rather race yourself? Stake from $5 to $500. Finish and it all comes back: the card part to your card, the Vault part to your Vault. Equip up to two Shields to cover a bad day; unused ones come back. Missed a race? One Comeback within 72 hours wins back half.

MEET THE CREW
Steppie is a lion in running shoes who runs your laps on the track. Dash the cheetah paces you all day. Shelly the tortoise keeps your Shields. Pip the sparrow brings the news. Bo the bear guards the Vault.

EVERY CENT IN VIEW
The Vault shows every entry, win, refund and cash-out. Cash out from $5, up to $500 a day, through Whop. Card payments are handled by Whop; Steppie never sees your card number.

FAIR BY DESIGN
Steppie reads steps from Apple Health, so iPhone, Apple Watch, Garmin, Fitbit, Oura, Withings and Samsung all count. Typed-in steps don't. Data that looks impossible goes to a person, never to an automatic loss. Walked a day that didn't sync? Send a Health screenshot and a person checks it.

FREE, AND ACTUALLY USEFUL
Step track, streaks, weight trend, practice laps, widgets and share cards are free forever.

STEPPIE PRO
No service fee when your balance pays and a lower fee by card, up to 5 races and purses at once, one free public purse a month, your own solo races with any goal and length, pace and best-hours insights, the gold mane badge and the Stride S app icon. Pro never touches purse money: finishers split all of it, Pro or not.

HOW WE MAKE MONEY
A service fee on purse entries, missed solo stakes, Shields and Steppie Pro.

Paid races are for adults 18 and over, and only where the law allows them. Whether you win depends only on your steps. Steppie is a motivation tool, not medical advice. Calorie and fat figures are estimates.
