# Steppie, the Steppie lion

![Character sheet](renders/grin-character-sheet.png)

## Who he is

**Steppie** is a young lion in a cobalt sweatband with a volt stripe, wearing a race bib printed with the G-Track mark. His name is the first syllable of Steppie. He's the friend who texts "walk?" at 7pm: warm, a bit cheeky, always on your side. He is never a guilt machine.

Why a lion:
- A **mane** is a visible thing that grows, which makes it a natural progress meter.
- A group of lions is a **pride**, which is our future name for friend teams.
- A **roar** is a celebration everyone understands.
- Lions read as strong without being intimidating once drawn chibi.
- Warm gold and orange is complementary to Lane Cobalt, so he pops on every brand surface.

## Design rules

- **Geometry:** built only from ellipses, capsules, and béziers in a 400 × 460 canvas (`tools/grin.py`). The same data renders as SVG for marketing and as native SwiftUI paths in the app (`MascotArt.swift`, generated by `tools/export_swift.py`). Never draw Steppie by hand in another tool; change `grin.py` and re-export.
- **Silhouette:** the flame-tuft mane, the swept three-tuft forelock over the band, and the big chibi head (about 55% of his height). Recognisable as a black shape.
- **Colour:** fur `#FFBE3D`, shade `#F2A12A`, mane `#F0781E` / `#D9601A`, highlight `#FF9A3C`, muzzle `#FFEBC2`. Brand cobalt and volt appear only on his band and bib.
- **Moods:** happy, calm, cheer, roar, worried, sleep, wink, proud. **Poses:** idle, cheer, wave, hold, flex.
- **Mane levels:** 0 Cub, 1 Young lion, 2 Lion, 3 King of the track.

## Motion rules

| Where | Frequency | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| Today, widget, empty states | many times a day | Near-invisible: 1.5% breath (2.8 s), tail sway (2.2 s), blink every 2.6–5.2 s. No bouncing loops. |
| Tap on Steppie | user-initiated | Squash-and-stretch hop (keyframes, about 0.8 s), a new line, and a soft haptic. "Roooar!" adds a rigid haptic. |
| Goal crossed, race entered, finish, medal | rare | Hop + cheer or roar pose + confetti burst in brand colours + success haptic. |
| Welcome | once | Waves (0.32 s arm oscillation) beside "Hey, I'm Steppie." |
| Reduce Motion | always | No loops, hops, or confetti. Expressions cross-fade. |

Pose changes use the snap spring (damping 0.62), the same physics as the bib pins. Mood changes cross-fade the face in 160 ms.

## Voice

Specific and warm. Steppie uses real numbers ("3,520 to go. A 36-minute walk does it.") and never shames. Money comes up only when a stake is live ("€20 says we walk tonight. I'll pace you."). Lines rotate, so the app doesn't repeat itself (variable reward). Source: `Coach` in `Mascot.swift`.

## The engagement system

| Mechanic | Research behind it | How Steppie does it (honestly) |
|---|---|---|
| **Character that evolves with you** | Finch: the pet grows as you complete goals (D1/D7 retention 54%/37%) | **The mane grows with goal days in the last 14.** It never resets overnight: a miss just pauses growth. It's shown on Progress (with a 14-day meter), Today, and the widget. |
| **Mascot voice in notifications** | Duolingo: Duo turns system alerts into social prompts | At most one evening nudge, in Steppie's voice, and only when you're behind: "Steppie's lacing up. About 1,840 steps keeps your €20." |
| **Streak wager** | Duolingo: streak wagers lift day-14 retention by 14% | That's the core product: the stake. Steppie makes it feel like a team effort, not a bet. |
| **Forgiveness** | Duolingo: losing long streaks makes people quit, so they added repair | Grace days in races, a mane that decays slowly, and no shaming copy. |
| **Collectibles** | Milestone medals in fitness apps | **Trophy case:** 13 medals for distance (half marathon to 1,000 km), streaks, races finished, and seasonal events. Each is celebrated once with a full-screen moment. |
| **Seasonal campaigns** | Duolingo's event campaigns drive resurrection | **Walktober (Oct 2026):** finish any race in October, practice laps included, for a limited medal. It's a cosmetic reward with no money involved. Shown as a banner on Races, and on X and Discord. |
| **Widget presence** | Finch: the widget changes as the pet evolves | The medium widget shows Steppie at your mane level with today's line. |
| **Identity and status** | Paid cosmetics | **Steppie Pro** includes the Steppie app icon. |
| **Shareable moments** | Duolingo's organic social reach | Share cards with Steppie cheering, the medal share, and a Discord emoji pack. |

What we deliberately don't do: guilt-trip notifications, fake urgency, streak-loss threats tied to money, or turning Steppie into a game that distracts from walking.
